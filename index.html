I am a CS PhD student at Yale University, advised by Prof. Fan Zhang. Before that, I earned my bachelor's degree from the Yao Class at Tsinghua University in 2023, where I was advised by Prof. Yifan Song.

My research interests lie in applied cryptography, blockchain security, and mechanism design. I am currently working on privacy, verifiability, and secure delegation for AI systems.
